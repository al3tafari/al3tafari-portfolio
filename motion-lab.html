PROMPTS

Biblioteca para Google Flow

Usa el SVG/PNG del logo como referencia visual. Flow permite animar imágenes, usar ingredientes y trabajar con fotogramas iniciales y finales para transiciones.

01 — Logo reveal editorial

Entrada limpia para portada, reel o caso de estudio.

Use the uploaded a.estudiocreativo logo as the exact visual reference. Create a 6-second minimalist motion-graphics intro on a pure black background. Reconstruct the logo from four precise components, one after another, with controlled easing, subtle dimensional depth, soft neutral highlights, and a single aquamarine accent on the dot. No camera shake, no extra typography, no redesign of the symbol. End on the exact original logo composition and hold for 1 second. Premium editorial motion design, restrained and precise.

02 — Origami / material study

Conecta el símbolo con tu lenguaje de papel y construcción.

Animate the uploaded a.estudiocreativo logo as if it were made from precision-folded paper. Preserve the exact silhouette and proportions. Over 8 seconds, the three main forms gently unfold, rotate and settle into the final logo; the small dot arrives last. Matte paper, studio black background, soft directional light, realistic micro-shadows, elegant motion design, no hands, no extra objects, no text. The final frame must match the reference logo exactly.

03 — Fragment / reassemble

Identidad experimental para clips cortos.

Use the uploaded logo as the immutable starting composition. Create a 6-second high-end motion-graphics experiment where the logo briefly stabilizes, then disintegrates into hundreds of clean geometric fragments and tiny paper-like particles, drifting in slow controlled arcs, then reassembling into the exact original logo. Black background, grayscale material palette with a single aquamarine accent. No random morphing, no altered logo geometry, no extra symbols. End with the exact reference composition.

04 — Seamless loop

Clip sin corte para portfolio o redes.

Create a seamless 8-second loop using the uploaded a.estudiocreativo logo as the exact reference. Start with the logo centered and still. A subtle breathing motion begins, the aquamarine dot moves through a restrained orbital path, the main forms respond with tiny parallax shifts, then everything returns precisely to the initial frame. Minimal black environment, soft studio lighting, premium motion-design aesthetic, no camera movement, no text, no extra elements. First and last frame must visually match.

Para transiciones críticas, usa el logo como fotograma inicial y/o final y genera varias variantes. La disponibilidad de modelos, duración y resolución depende de las funciones activas en tu cuenta.
